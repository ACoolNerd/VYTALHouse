# VYTAL House Brand Governance

## Official status
The VYTAL House gold-and-black monogram, primary wordmark, app icon, circular seal, and approved horizontal, ultrawide, square, and story lockups constitute the official visual identity system, version 1.0.

## Canonical identity
- Primary wordmark: metallic-gold VYTAL House lockup on transparent or VYTAL Black.
- Primary icon: metallic-gold VH/house monogram.
- Official positioning line: **Renew • Restore • Recharge**.
- Deprecated line: **Recovery • Contouring • Longevity** must not be used in new work without written approval.

## Approval authority
All public-facing uses should be approved by VYTAL Group's designated brand owner. Vendors receive only the files required for the approved channel and may not redraw, recolor, distort, animate, sell, sublicense, or register the mark.

## File integrity
- Never screenshot the logo from a website, slide, or social post.
- Use supplied PNG, WebP, SVG, JPG, and ICO files.
- Metallic masters are raster artwork. Flat SVGs are approved one-color production silhouettes, not metallic-gradient vectors.
- Preserve aspect ratio and maintain at least one H-crossbar height of clear space.
- Minimum digital sizes: wordmark 160 px wide; monogram 32 px wide. Use the simplified flat SVG below 48 px.

## Repository distribution
`ACoolNerd/VYTALHouse/brand-system` is canonical. `VYTALHouseAI`, `VYTALHouse_Agent`, `VYTALHOUSEdotCOM`, `VYTALHouseBuildout`, and `vytalhouserecess` must consume the same tokens and approved assets instead of creating alternatives.

## Change control
Any new logo variation, tagline, color, icon, animation, or co-brand lockup requires:
1. Written request and business rationale.
2. Brand-owner approval.
3. Versioned source file and proof.
4. Manifest and changelog update.
5. Synchronized downstream repository release.

## Operating principle
**Rights • Disclosure • Proof** — document who owns each asset, what relationship is represented, and what evidence supports every public claim.
