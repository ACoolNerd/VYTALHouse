# VYTAL House Operations + Compliance Stack

This stack contains member agreements, liability waivers, privacy policies, terms, vendor agreements, SOPs, risk controls, and compliance readiness templates.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Medical/Clinical Gate**: Do not market, sell, or administer regulated therapies such as IV/NAD+, peptides, hormone programs, or hyperbaric protocols without licensed medical oversight, signed protocols, insurance, consent documentation, and healthcare counsel review.

---

## Member Journey + Required Controls
| Stage | Member Action | Company Control | System Record |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Lead** | Visits site, joins waitlist, requests tour. | Marketing disclaimers; privacy notice; lead consent. | CRM lead + source. |
| **Application** | Selects membership tier and completes intake. | Age gate, health disclosure, risk screening, cancellation policy. | Application + questionnaire. |
| **Approval** | Front desk / clinical review. | Eligibility check, contraindication workflow, medical escalation. | Approved/declined status + reviewer. |
| **Onboarding** | Signs membership agreement and waivers. | Payment method, recurring billing consent, facility rules. | Signed docs + Stripe customer ID. |
| **Booking** | Books recovery session. | Room/equipment scheduling, session limits, staff assignment. | Appointment + modality + staff. |
| **Session** | Checks in and completes service. | Safety checklist, consent confirmation, incident form if needed. | Session notes + utilization. |
| **Renewal** | Continues or cancels membership. | Renewal/cancellation policy, retention workflow. | Renewal/cancel reason. |

---

## 1. Member Agreement - Core Terms
- **Access Rights**: Booking rules, service credits, guest policy.
- **Billing**: Recurring payment authorization, failed payment process, cancellation windows, refund policy.
- **Code of Conduct**: Facility rules, no-harassment policy, photo/video restrictions, confidentiality in member spaces.
- **Disclaimers**: No guarantee of medical outcome; services subject to eligibility screening.
- **Disputes**: Mediation, governing law (Maryland), class action waiver.

---

## 2. Wellness Participation Agreement / Liability Waiver
- **Assumption of Risk**: Recovery, thermal, pressure, light, compression, float, and wellness modalities.
- **Medical Disclaimer**: VYTAL House does not replace emergency care or primary medical advice.
- **Emergency Authorization**: Consent to seek medical assistance if needed.
- **Screening**: Contraindication disclosure, medications/supplements disclosure, pregnancy/heart/lung/seizure/claustrophobia screening.
- **Release and Indemnity**: Standard liability release subject to state enforceability review.

---

## 3. Health Disclosure + Intake Questionnaire
- **Health History**: Current medications, allergies, prior surgeries, cardiovascular/respiratory concerns.
- **Screening Triggers**: Triggers for clinical review prior to hyperbaric, cryotherapy, IV/NAD+, peptides, or sauna/cold plunge.
- **Consent**: Consent to store information securely and use it for service eligibility and safety.

---

## 4. Privacy Policy - Draft Terms
- **Data Categories Collected**: Contact, billing, booking, wellness intake, website analytics, communications.
- **Use Purposes**: Membership administration, booking, payments, safety, marketing with consent, legal compliance.
- **Disclosures**: Payment processor (Stripe), booking platform, CRM, medical providers, and legal/compliance advisors.
- **Security**: Encryption, role-based access, MFA, audit logging, retention schedule.

---

## 5. Terms of Service - Draft Terms
- **Site/App Rules**: Account security, acceptable use, intellectual property ownership, user content license, disclaimers.
- **Restrictions**: No scraping, no reverse engineering, no misuse of booking systems, no false health information.
- **Limitation of Liability**: Standard limitation of liability terms subject to counsel review.

---

## 6. Data Security Policy
- **Access**: MFA required for admin, CRM, email, payment, database, cloud, and document vault.
- **Privilege**: Role-based access; least privilege; quarterly access reviews.
- **Encryption**: Encrypted storage and backups; password manager required; no shared accounts.
- **Incident Response**: Identify, contain, investigate, notify, remediate, and document.

---

## 7. Record Retention Policy
- **Corporate Records**: Permanent.
- **Tax & Financial**: 7 years or CPA-defined period.
- **Member Agreements & Waivers**: Minimum 7 years after membership ends or longer if required.
- **Incident Reports & Clinical Logs**: Per medical/legal requirements.
- **Vendor Contracts & Warranties**: Contract term + 7 years.

---

## 8. Vendor Agreement - Core Terms
- **Scope**: Equipment/services; installation, shipping, training, warranty, maintenance, and support SLAs.
- **Insurance & Indemnity**: Liability insurance requirements, indemnification, confidentiality, data security if vendor touches member data.
- **Commercials**: Pricing, payment milestones, acceptance testing, delays, replacement parts.

---

## 9. Independent Contractor Agreement
- **Services & Deliverables**: Clinical or operational contractor services, schedules, and reporting.
- **Compensation**: Retainer, hourly, or per-session payment structure.
- **IP Ownership**: VYTAL House retains 100% ownership of intellectual property generated during service delivery.
- **Confidentiality & Non-solicitation**: Strict confidentiality for member data and proprietary systems; non-solicitation of members or staff survives contract termination.
- **Termination**: 30-day notice without cause; immediate termination for cause or compliance breaches.
