# 16 - SBA 7(a) Loan Preparation Dossier

## Executive Summary
This dossier outlines the preparation package for a Small Business Administration (SBA) 7(a) loan application for VYTAL House LLC. The proposed financing is designed to fund the capital-intensive facility build-out and the procurement of clinical-grade recovery modalities in Maryland.

- **Proposed Loan Amount:** $1,200,000
- **Guarantors:** Kathy Ha (50% Founding Principal) and Chauncey Gardner (50% Founding Principal).
- **Structure:** VYTAL Trust (Sovereign trust holding entity) -> VYTAL Holdings LLC -> VYTAL Operations LLC (operating borrower entity). Both principals will provide personal guarantees as required for any individual owning 20% or more of the equity interest.
- **Filing Status:** Draft for professional CPA, lender, and legal review.

---

The loan proceeds will be allocated specifically to build-out, capital equipment, and initial working capital:

## Financial Feasibility & Debt Service
The business plan models a recurring monthly membership revenue (MRR) of $179,700 at target capacity (300 active members across Core, Elite, and Black tiers).

### Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR)
Assuming a standard 10-year term at an estimated 9.5% interest rate for the SBA 7(a) loan:
- **Estimated Annual Principal & Interest (P&I):** ~$156,000 ($13,000/month)
- **Target Monthly Net Operating Income (NOI) Projection:** ~$18,000 (after lease payments, staff costs, clinical licensing, and marketing expenses)
- **Projected DSCR:** **1.38x** (NOI / Monthly P&I)
- **Feasibility Rating:** Strong. Standard lender underwriting requires a minimum DSCR of 1.15x to 1.25x.

---

## SBA Underwriting Checklist
Lenders will require the following standard documents to process the SBA 7(a) application:

1. **SBA Form 1919** - Borrower Information Form (completed by Kathy Ha and Chauncey Gardner).
2. **SBA Form 413** - Personal Financial Statement (completed by both partners showing assets, liabilities, and net worth).
3. **Business Financial Statements:**
   - Pro Forma Balance Sheet and 2-Year Cash Flow Projections.
   - Accounts Receivable and Accounts Payable aging summaries.
4. **Legal & Entity Documents:**
   - Articles of Organization (Draft prepared in Document 04).
   - Operating Agreement specifying the VYTAL Trust hierarchy.
   - Certificate of Good Standing from the Maryland Department of Assessments and Taxation (SDAT).
5. **Lease Agreement:** Copy of the executed lease or letter of intent showing a lease term matching or exceeding the 10-year loan amortization period.
6. **Modality Invoices/Quotes:** Formal equipment quotes from verified suppliers (e.g., Oxycell, EleveHealth, VacuActiv).

---

## Compliance & Security Safeguards
- **Asset Liens:** The loan will be secured by a first lien on all business assets, including the high-value hyperbaric chambers and fitness modalities.
- **HIPAA Boundary:** No member health information or private records shall be disclosed to the lender. Financial audits will only include aggregated membership revenue and non-identifiable booking metrics.
- **Review Gate:** This dossier is a planning tool. Final loan terms, interest rates, personal guarantees, and tax filings must be finalized with a certified accountant and SBA-approved commercial lender.
