# 17 - SBA 8(a) Business Development Program Certification Strategy

## Overview
The SBA 8(a) Business Development program provides business development assistance and sole-source contracting opportunities for small disadvantaged businesses. This document outlines the strategic path for VYTAL House LLC to secure this certification.

- **Primary Candidate:** Kathy Ha, as a female minority entrepreneur, is the qualifying Socially and Economically Disadvantaged Individual (SEDI) for the certification.
- **Co-Founder:** Chauncey Gardner (50% partner).
- **Target Market Alignment:** Maryland federal installations, municipal wellness partnerships, and state agency employee health recovery contracts.
- **Filing Status:** Strategic planning draft for certification counsel review.

---

## The 51% Ownership and Control Strategy
To qualify for 8(a) status, the SBA requires that the business be at least **51% unconditionally owned and directly controlled** by one or more socially and economically disadvantaged individuals. Under the default 50/50 principal split between Kathy Ha and Chauncey Gardner, two alternative paths are proposed to satisfy SBA regulations:

### Option A: Voting Class Restructuring
VYTAL Holdings LLC will issue two classes of membership interest:
- **Class A Voting Units (51% held by Kathy Ha):** Gives Kathy Ha direct voting control over all major corporate decisions, board appointments, and operational policies.
- **Class B Non-Voting / Limited Voting Units (49% held by Chauncey Gardner):** Maintains equal financial distribution rights (50/50 capital split) while ensuring that Kathy Ha holds the required 51% voting control for regulatory compliance.

### Option B: Sole Managing Member Appointment
The VYTAL Trust and LLC Operating Agreements will designate Kathy Ha as the **Sole Managing Member and President** with unilateral authority to manage day-to-day business operations, signing power, hiring/firing, and capital expenditures. This establishes the necessary "direct control" parameter for SBA audits.

## Disadvantaged Status Criteria

### 1. Social Disadvantage Criteria
Kathy Ha must submit a personal narrative detailing experiences of social disadvantage based on her identity, covering:
- **Education:** Barriers in academic environments or access to professional networks.
- **Employment:** Underrepresentation or advancement obstacles in corporate wellness or clinical industries.
- **Business History:** Discrimination or disproportionate hurdles when securing initial funding or leasing properties.

### 2. Economic Disadvantage Criteria
Kathy Ha must demonstrate economic disadvantage, satisfying the following SBA thresholds:
- **Personal Net Worth:** Must be under **$850,000** (excluding equity in her primary residence and her ownership interest in the applicant business).
- **Adjusted Gross Income (AGI):** Three-year average AGI must be under **$400,000**.
- **Fair Market Value of All Assets:** Total assets must be under **$6.5 million**.

## Compliance and Safeguards
- **Review Gate:** 8(a) rules are strictly enforced. Any change in the ownership structure that dilutes Kathy Ha's control or transfers equity must be reported to the SBA immediately.
- **Operating Agreement Alignment:** The VYTAL Operating Agreement must contain specific clauses stating that Kathy Ha's control is unconditional and that she receives compensation commensurate with her role as President and Managing Member.
- **Counsel Review:** This strategy is for planning purposes. The finalized operating agreement and application package must be signed off by a qualified corporate attorney specializing in government contracting and SBA certifications.
