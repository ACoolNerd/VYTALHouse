# VYTAL House Ownership + Formation Stack

This stack contains conceptual templates for the trust, holding company, operating companies, intellectual property holding company, property company, resolutions, and founder agreements.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Professional Review Required**: These are conceptual templates. Do not file or sign until reviewed by estate/trust counsel, corporate counsel, healthcare counsel, CPA/tax strategist, insurance advisor, and IP/privacy counsel.

---

## Assumptions
- **Founders**: Kathy Ha and Chauncey Gardner (50% / 50% split unless revised)
- **Management**: Manager-managed LLCs with Kathy and Chauncey as initial managers.
- **Parent Structure**: VYTAL Trust -> VYTAL Holdings LLC -> Operations / IP / Property / SPVs
- **Jurisdiction**: Maryland baseline for VYTAL House operations.
- **Purpose**: Wellness membership platform, recovery/longevity club, partner ecosystem, IP ownership, real estate/facility holding.

## 1. Master Trust Agreement - Conceptual Form

### Article 1 - Name and Declaration of Trust
- **Trust Name**: VYTAL Trust.
- **Founders/Settlors**: Kathy Ha and Chauncey Gardner.
- **Effective Date**: [DATE].

### Article 2 - Purpose
- Hold intellectual property, operating companies, real estate interests, digital assets, investments, reserves, and governance rights.
- Protect continuity of the VYTAL House platform and future related entities.

### Article 3 - Trustees and Authority
- **Initial Trustees**: Kathy Ha and Chauncey Gardner.
- Trustees may sign formation documents, open bank accounts, appoint managers, approve budgets, and direct subsidiary entities.
- Trustees owe duties of loyalty, care, confidentiality, and recordkeeping.

### Article 4 - Trust Protector
- **Independent Protector**: [NAME].
- Powers include trustee replacement, administrative amendments, dispute resolution, continuity oversight, and beneficiary protection.

### Article 5 - Beneficiaries / Interest Classes
- Founder beneficiaries.
- Family/estate beneficiaries if applicable.
- Charitable beneficiaries if approved.
- Member interest holders only if documented by separate instrument.

### Article 6 - Trust Assets
- Schedule A includes cash, brand assets, websites/domains, software, trademarks, membership interests, and other contributed assets.
- All assets require valuation support and transfer documentation.

### Article 7 - Governance + Voting
- Major actions require unanimous approval of initial trustees/managers.
- Deadlock escalates to written negotiation, mediation, and trust protector recommendation/decision as counsel approves.

### Article 8 - Records and Reporting
- Trustee minutes, resolution register, asset register, contribution register, financial reports, and annual review required.

### Article 9 - Amendment, Revocation, and Termination
- Final provision depends on trust type selected by counsel.
- Administrative amendments may be permitted through trustee/protector approval.

### Article 10 - Governing Law
- State of Maryland (or other jurisdiction selected before final drafting).

## 5. Operating Agreement - Shared Core Terms
- **Members**: VYTAL Holdings LLC or direct 50/50 founders depending on final tax structure.
- **Managers**: Kathy Ha and Chauncey Gardner as initial managers.
- **Economics**: Kathy Ha 50%; Chauncey Gardner 50% split.
- **Major Decisions**: Unanimous approval required for new members, equity issuance, debt above threshold, asset sales, IP transfer, dissolution, or merger.
- **Ordinary Decisions**: Approved by assigned manager within budget and policy.
- **Transfer Restrictions**: Right of first refusal, company consent, and compliance with securities terms.
- **Deadlock**: Good-faith negotiation -> mediation -> protector/advisor mechanism.
- **Indemnification**: Managers and officers indemnified except in cases of fraud or gross negligence.

---

## 6. Initial Member / Manager Resolutions
- Approve formation and operating agreement of VYTAL Holdings LLC.
- Authorize EIN applications.
- Authorize opening bank accounts.
- Adopt fiscal year and accounting method.
- Approve IP assignment into VYTAL IP LLC.
- Authorize vendor quote requests and pre-development expenses.

---

## 7. Banking Resolution
- **Accounts**: Operating Account, Reserve Account, Tax Account, Trust Account.
- **Limits**:
  - *Under $5,000*: Single founder approval.
  - *$5,001–$25,000*: Dual approval.
  - *Over $25,000*: Unanimous approval plus written resolution.
- Monthly reconciliation and quarterly review required.

---

## 8. Capital Contribution Agreement
- Record cash, IP, software, domain, design, services, and equipment contributions.
- Require valuation support.
- Clarify whether contribution is capital, reimbursable expense, loan, or founder service credit.

---

## 9. Founder Vesting / Buy-Sell Agreement
- **Trigger Events**: Death, disability, divorce, voluntary departure, misconduct, insolvency, deadlock.
- **Valuation Formula**: Appraisal, revenue multiple, book value, or hybrid formula to be selected.
- **Payment Terms**: Cash at closing or installment note with security.
- Restrictive covenants and confidentiality survive exit.

---

## 10. Founder IP Assignment Agreement
- Assign brand, logos, trade dress, code, designs, media, content, data models, websites, and social templates to VYTAL Intellectual Property LLC.
- Include warranty of originality and no third-party infringement.

---

## 11. Confidentiality + NDA
- Protect member data, vendor pricing, trade secrets, investor materials, financials, medical/clinical information, technology stack, and owner information.
- Applies to founders, staff, contractors, vendors, investors, and advisors.
